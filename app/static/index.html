🌴 Arizona Deal Agent

Finds the lowest-cost, most profitable, most affordable deal for your budget.

How to use

  1. Set your cash Budget ($).
  2. Slide toward Affordability (leftover cash) or Profit (fatter margin).
  3. Click a scenario or Rank deals.
  4. Take the green recommendation. Faded rows are over budget and are never picked.

Empty deal lists use the built-in Arizona sample catalog. Same ranking is available as python -m app and POST /api/rank.
